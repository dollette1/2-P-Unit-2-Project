Natasha Land

Why you should travel to Natasha Land

  • The ruler (Natasha) is benevolent
  • You can see the wonderful architecture
  • The world is imbued with magic
  • You can see rainbows every day and aurora boreali every night

Imagine living in Natasha Land

You wake up to the soft song of birds. You leave your house and look up at the castles built up brick by brick, adorned by detailed statues and stained glass galore. You look further up and see mansions that float into the bright blue of the sky and bob between the clouds. The children rush to the Academy, the oldest, most elite magical school ever created. Feel like taking a day trip? There are diverse regions with different climate zones at your fingertips. At the center of Natasha Land is a castle conjured by no other than Natasha herself. It is home to the greatest literature, art, and performances in the known universe.
